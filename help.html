Crisis support

If you might act on thoughts of suicide, or you can't keep yourself safe right now: call your emergency number (999, 911, 112) or go to the nearest emergency department.

If you are having suicidal thoughts but are safe for the moment: contact a crisis line today. Don't wait for your period to start or for the feelings to ease.

Suicidal thoughts can intensify around the menstrual cycle. They need prompt support, even if similar thoughts eased after previous periods.

PMDD peer support

The International Association for Premenstrual Disorders (IAPMD) offers peer support and resources: iapmd.org. Peer support does not replace crisis care.
