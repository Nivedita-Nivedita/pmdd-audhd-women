Crisis and support

If you are thinking about suicide or are in danger, call your local emergency number now, or go to your nearest emergency department.

PMDD can bring intense, short-lived thoughts of suicide in the days before a period. They are a symptom, they pass, and they deserve urgent care.

Find a crisis line

Crisis lines differ by country. Search for "crisis line" plus your country, or ask your doctor or local emergency service for the number. We will add verified numbers by country here.

PMDD peer support

The International Association for Premenstrual Disorders (IAPMD) runs peer support and resources for PMDD: iapmd.org.
