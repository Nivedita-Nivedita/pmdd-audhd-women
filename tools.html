Assessment tools

Questionnaires used in research and clinics. They can help you prepare for an assessment; none of them can diagnose you.

An adult ADHD or autism diagnosis needs a clinician, your childhood history and ideally someone who knew you then. PMDD needs at least two cycles of daily ratings: see Tracking your cycle with the DRSP.
