About

PMDD & AuDHD in Women brings together research on PMDD, ADHD, autism and the connected body in women: hormones, the gut, the immune system, sleep, sensory load, burnout and life stages.

Evidence grades

  • Established

    Consensus statements, meta-analyses or large replicated studies agree.

  • Supported

    Replicated findings, with limits in size, populations or methods.

  • Preliminary

    Small, single or early studies. Plausible, not settled.

  • Unsupported

    Widely repeated, but research does not back it or has not tested it.

A guide takes the grade of its weakest essential claim.

How papers are checked

Every paper's DOI is checked against Crossref. Every summary, number and conclusion is checked against the paper's abstract and conclusions, or its legally open full text when there is no abstract. 559 of 561 papers are verified this way; the others are original questionnaire papers with no abstract, used only as references.

Beta

The guides have not yet been reviewed by clinicians. They are for education, not diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a qualified clinician before changing anything about your care.

Corrections

Science changes and mistakes happen. Each guide ends with a corrections log, and every change to the site is kept in its public history.

Privacy
