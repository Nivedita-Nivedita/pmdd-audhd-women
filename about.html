About this site

PMDD & AuDHD in Women brings together research on PMDD, ADHD, autism and the connected body in women: hormones, the gut, the immune system, sleep, sensory load, burnout and life stages.

Who writes it

Compiled by a scientist with lived experience of late-identified neurodivergence and cyclical symptoms. The author is anonymous for now. AI tools helped search, check and draft; every factual statement is tied to a published paper, and every draft is marked for clinician review.

How we grade evidence

  • Established Consensus statements, meta-analyses or large replicated studies agree.
  • Supported Replicated findings, but with limits (size, populations, methods).
  • Preliminary Small, single or early studies. Promising or plausible, not settled.
  • Popular, unsupported Widely repeated, but research does not back it (or has not tested it).

A guide takes the grade of its weakest essential claim.

How we check papers

Every paper's DOI is checked against Crossref. Every summary, number and conclusion is checked against the paper's abstract and conclusions, or its legally open full text when no abstract exists. 559 of 561 papers are verified this way; the rest are original questionnaire papers with no abstract anywhere, used only as references.

Beta status

The guides have not yet been reviewed by clinicians. They are for education, not diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a qualified clinician before changing anything about your care.

Corrections

Science changes and we make mistakes. Each guide ends with a corrections log, and every change is recorded in the site's public history.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It loads no third-party scripts or fonts.
