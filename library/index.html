Research library

609 peer-reviewed papers, each with a plain-language summary.

How these were checked: each paper's details were matched to its DOI record, and each summary was compared with the paper's abstract and conclusions (or its open full text where there is no abstract). This confirms we summarised the abstract fairly; it does not replace reading the full paper, and abstracts often leave out eligibility, harms and subgroup details. Summaries have not yet been reviewed by clinicians. Grades on papers describe our rating of the study's design and quality, not a recommendation.
