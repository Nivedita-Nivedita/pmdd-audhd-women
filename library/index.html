Research library

627 peer-reviewed papers, each with a plain-language summary, newest first.

How these were checked: each paper's details were matched to its DOI record, and each summary was compared with the paper's abstract and conclusions (or its open full text where there is no abstract). This confirms we summarised the abstract fairly; it does not replace reading the full paper, and abstracts often leave out eligibility, harms and subgroup details. Summaries have not yet been reviewed by clinicians. Each paper is labelled with its study design (the kind of study). That is not a quality score: a small trial can be weak and a qualitative study can be the best evidence for a question about experience. Confidence ratings belong to specific claims, in the guides and Claim check.
