Books and guidelines

No open-access book on diagnosing ADHD in adult women exists yet. These are the best free guidelines and collections, and some well-known paid books.

Open-access books and book chapters

  • ADHD: New Directions in Diagnosis and Treatment

    an edited academic volume covering comorbidity, biomarkers, executive and social functioning, and ADHD in college students. Not focused on women, but has adult-relevant chapters.

    2015, IntechOpen

  • Knowing and Understanding ADHD in the Light of New Scientific Evidence and Research Suggestion

    recent chapters on co-occurring conditions, sleep, diagnosis updates, and AI in diagnosis. Mostly about children and adolescents, so of limited relevance for adult women.

    2025 (29 Oct), IntechOpen

  • Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (Chapter D.1, IACAPAP e-Textbook of Child and Adolescent Mental Health)

    a thorough, citable overview of diagnosis and treatment. Its focus is children, with some lifespan context.

    2020, International Association for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Allied Professions

  • ADHD and Substance Use Disorders in Adults: A Systematic Review (VA Evidence Synthesis Program)

    evidence on stimulant safety and SUD risk in adults with ADHD. Useful context for adult treatment decisions.

    Nov 2024, U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, hosted on NCBI Bookshelf

  • Autistic Community and the Neurodiversity Movement: Stories from the Frontline

    the history and thinking behind the neurodiversity movement. It is about autism, not ADHD, so treat it as background for the neurodivergent-identity framing only.

    2020, Palgrave Macmillan (Springer Nature Singapore)

  • Neurodiversity Studies: A New Critical Paradigm

    critical neurodiversity theory covering wellbeing, work, and communication. Not specific to ADHD.

    2020, Routledge (Routledge Advances in Sociology)

  • Critical Neurodiversity Studies: Divergent Textualities in Literature and Culture

    neurodivergent perspectives in literature and culture. Low relevance for clinical diagnosis.

    2025, Bloomsbury Academic (Critical Interventions in the Medical and Health Humanities)

  • The Palgrave Handbook of Critical Menstruation Studies

    chapter 23 is "Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS) and the Myth of the Irrational Female" by Sally King, a critical history of how PMS/PMDD came to be diagnosed. Useful for the hormones and gender-bias angle. It is not a clinical guide to PMDD.

    2020, Palgrave Macmillan

  • Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD): Diagnosis, Treatment, and Management

    **one of the closest matches to the top priority.** The special issue focuses on the adult pathway (pre-diagnosis, referral, diagnosis, treatment) and refining ADHD criteria for women, including post-menopausal women and female-specific treatment.

    Jan 2025, MDPI Books

Free clinical guidelines and official reports

  • Females with ADHD: An expert consensus statement taking a lifespan approach providing guidance for the identification and treatment of ADHD in girls and women

    **the single most important free document for the top priority.** It covers why women are under-recognised, how their presentation differs, masking, comorbidity, hormonal transitions, and specific recommendations for identifying and assessing women.

    2020, *BMC Psychiatry* 20:404 (Springer Nature / BMC)

  • NICE CG72 full guideline: Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder: Diagnosis and Management of ADHD in Children, Young People and Adults

    **the full evidence base behind UK adult diagnostic practice.** NG87 (2018) did not re-review diagnostic criteria, so the diagnosis reasoning still lives here. Includes chapters on adult diagnosis and the experience of care.

    2009 (guideline issued 2008), British Psychological Society and Royal College of Psychiatrists

  • NICE NG87: Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: diagnosis and management

    the UK's current standard. Section 1.3 covers adult diagnosis and says adults should be referred to a specialist.

    2018, last updated Sept 2019

  • NICE NG87 evidence reviews on NCBI Bookshelf (2018)

  • Canadian ADHD Practice Guidelines (CAP-Guidelines), 4.1 Edition

    **especially useful in Canada.** It includes adult assessment toolkit forms (e.g. CADDRA adult assessment and ASRS). Whether it has a dedicated section on women is UNVERIFIED. No 5th edition had been announced on the page as of this search.

    January 2020

  • Australian Evidence-Based Clinical Practice Guideline for ADHD

    a recent, comprehensive, lifespan guideline that includes identification and diagnosis in adults. It considers sex and gender, but how much it says about women specifically is UNVERIFIED.

    Oct 2022

  • ADHD in adults: Good practice guidance (College Report CR235)

    a practical guide for general psychiatrists assessing and diagnosing adults with ADHD.

    Jan 2023

  • Updated European Consensus Statement on diagnosis and treatment of adult ADHD

    **a core reference for adult diagnosis.** Covers how symptoms present in adults, differential diagnosis, and gender and lifespan issues, including women and hormonal influences.

    2019, *European Psychiatry* 56:14–34

  • The World Federation of ADHD Guide

    a short book-length clinical guide covering causes, neurobiology, assessment across the lifespan, and treatment.

    2019, Artmed / World Federation of ADHD

  • The World Federation of ADHD International Consensus Statement: 208 Evidence-based Conclusions about the Disorder

    an evidence summary you can cite to answer "is ADHD real or overdiagnosed" arguments. Includes conclusions on sex differences.

    2021, *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 128:789–818

  • DIVA-5: Diagnostic Interview for ADHD in Adults

    the standard semi-structured interview for diagnosing adults, covering all 18 DSM-5 criteria in both childhood and adulthood. Listed here so you know what clinicians use.

  • Report of the independent ADHD Taskforce (Parts 1 and 2)

    the current UK policy direction on adult assessment, waiting lists and primary-care diagnosis. Whether it says anything specific about women is UNVERIFIED.

    interim report June 2025; final report (Part 2) 6 Nov 2025; NHS England

  • ADHD Diagnosis and Treatment in Children and Adolescents (AHRQ Comparative Effectiveness Review No. 267)

    **children only**, so of limited use for adult women. Its diagnosis chapter is still a rigorous review of how accurate assessment tools are.

    Mar 2024, AHRQ

  • IAPMD Premenstrual Disorders (PMDs) Treatment Guidelines

    covers clinical treatment of PMDD and PME (premenstrual exacerbation of another condition, such as ADHD). The DRSP is the gold-standard tracker used to diagnose PMDD.

    UNVERIFIED

  • RCOG Green-top Guideline No. 48: Management of Premenstrual Syndrome

    the UK clinical standard for PMS/PMDD.

    2016, published in *BJOG* 2017; second edition

Frontiers and journal e-book collections

  • Women in Psychiatry 2023: ADHD — e-book confirmed (PDF and EPUB)

    includes "Female-specific pharmacotherapy in ADHD: premenstrual adjustment of psychostimulant dosage", "Female sex and burden of depressive symptoms predict insufficient response to telemedical treatment in adult ADHD", CBT in adults, and an umbrella review of the impacts of ADHD.

  • ADHD in Women and Girls — still open; no e-book yet

    **directly on topic: hormones, ADHD medication across the cycle, trauma overlap.** The key review is Research advances and future directions in female ADHD: the lifelong interplay of hormonal fluctuations with mood, cognition, and disease, *Front. Glob. Womens Health* 2025, doi 10.3389/fgwh.2025.1613628: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/global-womens-health/articles/10.3389/fgwh.2025.1613628/full

  • Adult ADHD and other Psychiatric Disorders — e-book availability UNVERIFIED

    recognising ADHD in adults when it overlaps with mood and anxiety disorders, which is central to misdiagnosis in women. Includes "The lived experiences of adults with ADHD: a rapid review".

  • Psychiatric Illness Across the Menstrual Cycle — e-book confirmed (PDF and EPUB)

    PMDD, premenstrual exacerbation (PME) of psychiatric conditions, and PMDD and suicidality. It doesn't mention ADHD directly, but PME is the framework for cycle-linked worsening of ADHD symptoms.

  • The Trajectory of ADHD Across the Lifespan — open for submissions until Jan 2027; no e-book
