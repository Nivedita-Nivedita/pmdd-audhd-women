Guidelines and clinical references

Official guidelines, classifications, prescribing and lactation references, and patient information that the guides rely on alongside research papers. These are kept separate from the research library because they are a different kind of source: recommendations and reference information rather than studies.

Clinical guidelines

  • Antenatal and postnatal mental health: clinical management and service guidance (CG192)

    G26, G27, G28: perinatal mood screening questions, referral of severe mental illness, postpartum psychosis assessment within 4 hours, specialist advice on psychotropic medicines in breastfeeding; last updated 11 February 2020

    National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE), 2020 · checked 2026-10-05

  • Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: diagnosis and management (NG87), Recommendations 1.7.4-1.7.5 (baseline cardiovascular assessment, cardiology referral) and 1.8.9-1.8.11 (heart rate and blood pressure monitoring)

    G38/G37: baseline pulse/BP and cardiovascular assessment before ADHD medication, cardiology referral triggers, monitoring before/after dose changes and every 6 months, action on sustained resting tachycardia or arrhythmia

    National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE), 2018 · checked 2026-10-05

  • Endometriosis: diagnosis and management (NG73), Recommendations 1.3.1-1.3.4 (when to suspect), 1.5.5-1.5.7 (referral)

    G09: symptoms that should prompt suspicion of endometriosis, symptom diary, neurodivergence affecting pain expression, when to refer to gynaecology/specialist services

    National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE), 2024 · checked 2026-10-05

  • Management of Premenstrual Disorders (ACOG Clinical Practice Guideline No. 7)

    Diagnosis and treatment of PMS and PMDD.

    American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists, 2023 · checked 2026-10-05

  • Menopause: identification and management (NG23)

    G23, G30, G34: HRT indications (vasomotor and menopause-related depressive symptoms) and risks (VTE with oral HRT, breast cancer with combined HRT, stroke with oral oestrogen, dementia if combined HRT started at 65 or over); last updated 15 April 2026

    National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE), 2026 · checked 2026-10-05

Regulatory and prescribing information

  • Acetylcysteine solution (Hospira), prescribing information: Warnings, Precautions and Adverse Reactions

    G24: harms note for NAC (nausea, vomiting, rare rash/urticaria with oral use; bronchospasm with inhaled use in asthma; caution where gastric bleeding risk); label is for prescription uses, not for BFRBs or supplements

    US Food and Drug Administration label via DailyMed (National Library of Medicine), 2026 · checked 2026-10-05

  • CONCERTA (methylphenidate HCl) extended-release tablets, prescribing information, Warnings 5.2 (Risks to patients with serious cardiac disease) and 5.3 (Increased blood pressure and heart rate); 12.1 (Mechanism of action)

    G38/G37: CNS stimulants raise mean heart rate about 3-6 bpm, some patients have larger increases; monitor all patients for hypertension and tachycardia; avoid in serious cardiac disease; label states methylphenidate blocks norepinephrine and dopamine reuptake (relevant to HRS class recommendation)

    US Food and Drug Administration label via DailyMed (National Library of Medicine), 2026 · checked 2026-10-05
