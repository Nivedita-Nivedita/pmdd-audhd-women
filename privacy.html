Privacy notice

What this site stores, what it sends, and how we checked.

Browsing the site

  • No cookies, analytics or trackers. Pages, fonts, styles and data files all load from this site; there are no third-party scripts.
  • Stored only in your browser, and only if you choose: your light or dark setting, and on the symptom pages your ticks, but only if you turn on "Remember my ticks". They stay on your device until you press "Delete my ticks" or clear your browser data. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

The Share with us form

  • What is sent: only what you enter: your message, and optionally a link or DOI, the tags you tick, your name and your email address. The form also sends your consent tick.
  • Who receives it: the form is processed by Formspree, a form service based in the United States, which delivers it to Nivedita by email. Formspree says it may also record technical details such as your IP address, browser type and access time, and that it keeps information only as long as necessary for its stated purposes.
  • How it is used: only to improve this site and, if you gave an email address, to reply. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.
  • Deleting it: to ask for a message to be deleted, send a new message through the form that says so, including enough detail to find the original.
  • Please don't include health details about yourself or anyone else, and don't use the form in an emergency. See crisis support.

How we checked

On 5 October 2026 we loaded the home, library, symptom and Claim check pages in a browser and recorded every network request: all came from this site, no cookies were set and nothing was saved in browser storage until a setting was chosen. We did not inspect Formspree's or GitHub's internal systems; for those we rely on their published policies.
