Topics

11 connected areas of research, from core ADHD to hormones, the gut, sleep and life stages.

This map is a work in progress: we add topics and links as the research grows, and only draw a link when studies in our library support it.

All 19 links and the evidence behind them
  • Core ADHD ↔ Autism & burnout Established

    ADHD and autism often occur together: 28-39% of autistic people studied had ADHD.

    Main sources: Lai 2019; Rong 2021

  • Core ADHD ↔ Emotion Established

    Emotional dysregulation is common and impairing in children and adults with ADHD.

    Main sources: Beheshti 2020; Faraone 2018

  • Immune ↔ Core ADHD Established

    Asthma and eczema are modestly more common in children with ADHD; cause unknown.

    Main sources: Cortese 2018; Kim 2020

  • Autism & burnout ↔ Emotion Supported

    About half of autistic people studied had difficulty identifying their emotions (alexithymia).

    Main sources: Kinnaird 2019

  • Body ↔ Core ADHD Supported

    Autism and ADHD are several times more common in people with Ehlers-Danlos syndrome or hypermobility.

    Main sources: Cederlöf 2016; Csecs 2022

  • Core ADHD ↔ Hormones Supported

    Women with ADHD screen positive for PMDD more often than women without ADHD (not yet confirmed with daily ratings).

    Main sources: Broughton 2025; Tsuji 2026; Groenman 2026

  • Giftedness ↔ Core ADHD Supported

    ADHD occurs at every IQ level; bright people with ADHD still have real impairment.

    Main sources: Antshel 2007; Rommelse 2016

  • Gut & pelvis ↔ Core ADHD Supported

    IBS and constipation are more common in people with ADHD.

    Main sources: Blake 2026; Kedem 2020

  • Gut & pelvis ↔ Iron Supported

    Heavy menstrual bleeding is a common cause of iron deficiency.

    Main sources: Munro 2023

  • Hormones ↔ Emotion Supported

    Mood can worsen before periods: PMDD, or premenstrual exacerbation of another condition.

    Main sources: Eisenlohr-Moul 2017; Kuehner 2021

  • Iron ↔ Core ADHD Supported

    Ferritin is slightly lower on average in children with ADHD; low iron is not shown to cause ADHD.

    Main sources: Wang 2017; Tseng 2018

  • Life stages ↔ Hormones Supported

    A history of premenstrual disorders is linked with perinatal depression.

    Main sources: Yang 2024

  • Life stages ↔ Core ADHD Supported

    Girls with ADHD are diagnosed later and less often than boys.

    Main sources: Mowlem 2018; Martin 2024

  • Sleep ↔ Core ADHD Supported

    Many people with ADHD have a later body clock (delayed melatonin onset).

    Main sources: Van Veen 2010; Coogan 2017

  • Body ↔ Hormones Preliminary

    POTS symptoms may vary across the menstrual cycle.

    Main sources: Peggs 2012; Fu 2010

  • Body ↔ Iron Preliminary

    Low iron stores were common in teenagers with POTS (one small study).

    Main sources: Jarjour 2013

  • Giftedness ↔ Autism & burnout Preliminary

    Camouflaging is common in autism, also reported in ADHD, and linked with poorer mental health.

    Main sources: Livingston 2019; Cassidy 2018; Cook 2021

  • Hormones ↔ Immune Preliminary

    Asthma and eczema often worsen before periods; inflammation markers linked with PMS in small studies.

    Main sources: Sánchez-Ramos 2016; McSwiney 2025; Granda 2021; Gold 2016

  • Immune ↔ Gut & pelvis Preliminary

    Women with ADHD had higher odds of Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis (one register study).

    Main sources: Hegvik 2018

  • ADHD in women

    ADHD in women is under-recognised rather than a different disorder.

  • Giftedness & masking

    High IQ can hide ADHD. It does not protect against it.

  • Emotion & rejection sensitivity

    Emotional dysregulation is a core feature of adult ADHD. 'RSD' is not a validated construct.

  • Hormones & PMDD

    PMDD is a different brain response to normal hormone changes. Women with ADHD screen positive for it more often.

  • Immune system & allergy

    Allergy and autoimmune links are real but modest. Neuroinflammation is still a hypothesis.

  • Gut & pelvis

    Functional gut problems such as IBS are more common with ADHD; a link with endometriosis is preliminary.

  • Iron & energy

    Iron helps fatigue in menstruating women even without anaemia.

  • Circadian rhythm & sleep

    A late body clock is measurable in adult ADHD.

  • Body & daily function

    Daily-life impairment, eating, sensory load and repetitive behaviours are part of the picture.

  • Autism overlap, sensory & burnout

    ADHD and autism often co-occur, and women and girls with either are under-identified.

  • Life stages & populations

    Pregnancy, new motherhood, menopause, adolescence, culture and gender all change how ADHD and autism show up and get recognised.
