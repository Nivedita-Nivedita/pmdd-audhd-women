Topics / Iron & energy

Iron & energy

Iron helps fatigue in menstruating women even without anaemia.

What is solid

  • Randomised trials show iron reduces fatigue in non-anaemic menstruating women with low ferritin (Vaucher 2012, Verdon 2003).

What is thin or missing

  • Ferritin is only slightly lower in ADHD. 'Low iron causes ADHD' is overstated.

Claims checked

Untested   Preliminary

Popular claim, paraphrased
Low iron (ferritin) causes POTS, and iron fixes it.

In 24 adolescents with POTS, half had low iron stores (ferritin 25 μg/L or less) vs 14% in the US paediatric population (Jarjour 2013). People with POTS can have reduced red-cell and plasma volume (Raj 2005). We did not find a controlled study or treatment trial testing whether correcting iron improves POTS (search last updated October 2026). Anaemia is one of the conditions that must be excluded before POTS is diagnosed (Raj 2022).

Closer to the evidence: Low iron stores may be more common in teenagers with POTS and are worth checking with a clinician, but there is no trial evidence yet that iron treats POTS, and iron shouldn't be taken long term without monitoring.

Sources
  • Jarjour 2013
  • Raj 2005
  • Raj 2022

Overstated   Preliminary

Popular claim, paraphrased
Low iron (ferritin) causes ADHD.

In children, ferritin is modestly lower in ADHD (SMD -0.40 and g=-0.25 in two meta-analyses) but serum iron showed no clear difference (Wang 2017; Tseng 2018). These are associations; the evidence does not establish that low iron causes ADHD. A widely cited early finding of ferritin below 30 in 84% of children with ADHD (Konofal 2004) has not held up at that size, and the only placebo-controlled iron trial in ADHD had 23 children (Konofal 2008). Adult data are limited to a few small studies. Ferritin testing does not diagnose ADHD, and iron is not an established ADHD treatment.

Closer to the evidence: Ferritin is slightly lower on average in children with ADHD, but the evidence does not establish that low iron causes ADHD. Low iron is worth checking and treating for its own sake, with a clinician monitoring levels.

Sources
  • Wang 2017
  • Tseng 2018
  • Konofal 2004
  • Konofal 2008

Mostly true   Supported

Popular claim, paraphrased
Low ferritin can leave you exhausted even if you're not anaemic, and iron helps.

In 198 menstruating women with ferritin below 50 and normal haemoglobin, 12 weeks of iron reduced fatigue by 47.7% vs 28.8% on placebo (Vaucher 2012); an earlier trial found benefit only when ferritin was 50 or lower (Verdon 2003). A meta-analysis of 18 trials confirmed less self-reported fatigue (SMD -0.38) but no gain in objective physical capacity (Houston 2018). Iron deficiency is common: 38.6% of US females aged 12-21 had ferritin below 25 (Weyand 2023, verified against full text).

Closer to the evidence: Iron deficiency without anaemia is common in menstruating women and can cause fatigue that improves with iron; ask for ferritin, not just haemoglobin.

Sources
  • Vaucher 2012
  • Verdon 2003
  • Houston 2018
  • Weyand 2023

Key research

19 papers. A selection, listed by study design (reviews and trials first), then newest first. The label is the kind of study, not a quality score. See all in the library.

  • Evidence-based and consensus clinical practice guidelines for the iron treatment of restless legs syndrome/Willis-Ekbom disease in adults and children: an IRLSSG task force report

    Guidelines say iron treatment, especially through a drip, can help restless legs when iron stores are low.

    Allen 2018, Sleep Medicine · Guideline

  • Peripheral iron levels in children with attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder: a systematic review and meta-analysis

    Children with ADHD tend to have slightly lower iron stores, and low iron was linked to more severe symptoms.

    Tseng 2018, Scientific Reports · Meta-analysis

  • Efficacy of iron supplementation on fatigue and physical capacity in non-anaemic iron-deficient adults: a systematic review of randomised controlled trials

    In iron-deficient adults without anaemia, iron supplements eased tiredness a little but did not improve physical fitness.

    Houston 2018, BMJ Open · Meta-analysis

  • Iron Status in Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

    Children with ADHD tend to have lower iron stores (ferritin), though their serum iron did not differ.

    Wang 2017, PLOS ONE · Meta-analysis

  • Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder and restless leg syndrome across the lifespan: A systematic review and meta-analysis

    Restless legs symptoms are common in people with ADHD, affecting roughly a fifth to a third of adults.

    Migueis 2023, Sleep Medicine Reviews · Systematic review

  • Iron and attention deficit/hyperactivity disorder: what is the empirical evidence so far? A systematic review of the literature

    Studies on iron and ADHD are mixed, with only small treatment trials so far.

    Cortese 2012, Expert Review of Neurotherapeutics · Systematic review

  • Effect of iron supplementation on fatigue in nonanemic menstruating women with low ferritin: a randomized controlled trial

    Women with low iron stores but no anaemia felt less tired after taking iron tablets for 12 weeks.

    Vaucher 2012, Canadian Medical Association Journal · Randomised trial

  • Effects of Iron Supplementation on Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder in Children

    In a very small trial, iron tablets improved ADHD symptoms in children with low iron stores.

    Konofal 2008, Pediatric Neurology · Randomised trial

  • Iron supplementation for unexplained fatigue in non-anaemic women: double blind randomised placebo controlled trial

    Iron reduced unexplained tiredness in women, but only in those with low iron stores.

    Verdon 2003, BMJ · Randomised trial

  • Multimodal MR Imaging of Brain Iron in Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder: A Noninvasive Biomarker That Responds to Psychostimulant Treatment?

    A small brain-scan study found lower brain iron in unmedicated young people with ADHD.

    Adisetiyo 2014, Radiology · Case-control study

  • Iron Deficiency in Children With Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder

    In a small study, most children with ADHD had low iron stores compared with other children.

    Konofal 2004, Archives of Pediatrics & Adolescent Medicine · Case-control study

  • Prevalence of Iron Deficiency and Iron-Deficiency Anemia in US Females Aged 12-21 Years, 2003-2020

    About 4 in 10 US teenage girls and young women had low iron stores, and about 6 in 100 had iron-deficiency anaemia.

    Weyand 2023, JAMA · Cross-sectional study
