Iron & energy

Iron helps fatigue in menstruating women even without anaemia.

What is solid

  • Randomised trials show iron reduces fatigue in non-anaemic menstruating women with low ferritin (Vaucher 2012, Verdon 2003).

What is thin or missing

  • Ferritin is only slightly lower in ADHD. 'Low iron causes ADHD' is overstated.

How it connects

  • Gut & pelvis: Endometriosis and heavy bleeding drain iron stores Supported
  • Body & daily function: Low ferritin adds to fatigue; iron helps Supported
  • Body & daily function: Low iron stores reported in adolescents with POTS Preliminary

Claims checked

Unsupported Preliminary

“Low iron (ferritin) causes POTS, and iron fixes it.”

In 24 adolescents with POTS, half had low iron stores (ferritin 25 μg/L or less) vs 14% in the US paediatric population (Jarjour 2013). People with POTS can have reduced red-cell and plasma volume (Raj 2005). No controlled study or treatment trial tests whether correcting iron improves POTS.

A more accurate way to say it: Low iron stores may be more common in teenagers with POTS and are worth checking, but there is no evidence yet that iron treats POTS.

Sources
  • Jarjour 2013
  • Raj 2005

Overstated Preliminary

“Low iron (ferritin) causes ADHD.”

In children, ferritin is modestly lower in ADHD (SMD -0.40 and g=-0.25 in two meta-analyses) but serum iron does not differ (Wang 2017; Tseng 2018). A widely cited early finding of ferritin below 30 in 84% of children with ADHD (Konofal 2004) has not held up at that size, and the only placebo-controlled iron trial in ADHD had 23 children (Konofal 2008). Adult data are almost absent.

A more accurate way to say it: Ferritin is slightly lower on average in children with ADHD; low iron is worth checking and treating, but it doesn't cause ADHD.

Sources
  • Wang 2017
  • Tseng 2018
  • Konofal 2004
  • Konofal 2008

Mostly true Supported

“Low ferritin can leave you exhausted even if you're not anaemic, and iron helps.”

In 198 menstruating women with ferritin below 50 and normal haemoglobin, 12 weeks of iron reduced fatigue by 47.7% vs 28.8% on placebo (Vaucher 2012); an earlier trial found benefit only when ferritin was 50 or lower (Verdon 2003). A meta-analysis of 18 trials confirmed less self-reported fatigue (SMD -0.38) but no gain in objective physical capacity (Houston 2018). Iron deficiency is common: 38.6% of US females aged 12-21 had ferritin below 25 (Weyand 2023, verified against full text).

A more accurate way to say it: Iron deficiency without anaemia is common in menstruating women and can cause fatigue that improves with iron; ask for ferritin, not just haemoglobin.

Sources
  • Vaucher 2012
  • Verdon 2003
  • Houston 2018
  • Weyand 2023

Key research

19 papers in this topic, strongest evidence first. Browse them all in the library.

  • Iron Deficiency in Adults Auerbach 2025, JAMA Established
    Low iron can cause tiredness and poor concentration and is diagnosed with a blood test for iron stores.
  • Prevalence of Iron Deficiency and Iron-Deficiency Anemia in US Females Aged 12-21 Years, 2003-2020 Weyand 2023, JAMA Established
    About 4 in 10 US teenage girls and young women had low iron stores, and about 6 in 100 had iron-deficiency anaemia.
  • Iron deficiency Pasricha 2021, The Lancet Established
    Low iron can affect how you feel and function even before it causes anaemia.
  • Evidence-based and consensus clinical practice guidelines for the iron treatment of restless legs syndrome/Willis-Ekbom disease in adults and children: an IRLSSG task force report Allen 2018, Sleep Medicine Established
    Guidelines say iron treatment, especially through a drip, can help restless legs when iron stores are low.
  • Iron deficiency anaemia Lopez 2016, The Lancet Established
    A review of iron-deficiency anaemia: who gets it, how it is tested for and how it is treated.
  • The relationship between heavy menstrual bleeding, iron deficiency, and iron deficiency anemia Munro 2023, American Journal of Obstetrics and Gynecology Supported
    Heavy periods are a common, under-recognised cause of low iron and anaemia.
  • Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder and restless leg syndrome across the lifespan: A systematic review and meta-analysis Migueis 2023, Sleep Medicine Reviews Supported
    Restless legs symptoms are common in people with ADHD, affecting roughly a fifth to a third of adults.
  • Restless legs syndrome and iron deficiency in adults with attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder Lopez 2019, Sleep Supported
    About a third of adults with ADHD had restless legs syndrome, often alongside low iron stores.
  • Effect of iron supplementation on fatigue in nonanemic menstruating women with low ferritin: a randomized controlled trial Vaucher 2012, Canadian Medical Association Journal Supported
    Women with low iron stores but no anaemia felt less tired after taking iron tablets for 12 weeks.
  • Iron supplementation for unexplained fatigue in non-anaemic women: double blind randomised placebo controlled trial Verdon 2003, BMJ Supported
    Iron reduced unexplained tiredness in women, but only in those with low iron stores.
  • Peripheral iron levels in children with attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder: a systematic review and meta-analysis Tseng 2018, Scientific Reports Supported
    Children with ADHD tend to have slightly lower iron stores, and low iron was linked to more severe symptoms.
  • Efficacy of iron supplementation on fatigue and physical capacity in non-anaemic iron-deficient adults: a systematic review of randomised controlled trials Houston 2018, BMJ Open Supported
    In iron-deficient adults without anaemia, iron supplements eased tiredness a little but did not improve physical fitness.
