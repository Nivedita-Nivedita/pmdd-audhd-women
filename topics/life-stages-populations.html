Topics / Life stages & populations

Life stages & populations

Pregnancy, new motherhood, menopause, adolescence, culture and gender all change how ADHD and autism show up and get recognised.

What is solid

  • ADHD medication in pregnancy is largely reassuring in large registry studies; modafinil is the exception (see G26).
  • A history of premenstrual disorder strongly predicts depression around pregnancy, and the link runs both ways (Yang 2024).
  • New ADHD diagnoses in mothers peak 2–5 years after childbirth in Danish registers.
  • ADHD is under-diagnosed in US Black, Hispanic and especially Asian-American children and adults.

What is thin or missing

  • Breastfeeding on stimulants has only case-level data.
  • Women over 60 are not reported separately in any study found.
  • Stigma and help-seeking in South Asian and Indian women are unstudied.
  • ADHD in trans people is barely studied.

How it connects

  • Hormones & PMDD: Premenstrual disorder history predicts perinatal depression Supported
  • ADHD in women: Diagnosis delayed in girls and peaks in mothers after childbirth Supported

Key research

90 papers. A selection, listed by study design (reviews and trials first), then newest first. The label is the kind of study, not a quality score. See all in the library.

  • Initial Clinical Guidelines for Co-Occurring Autism Spectrum Disorder and Gender Dysphoria or Incongruence in Adolescents

    Experts agreed that autistic teenagers questioning their gender need careful, longer assessment and support across several areas of life.

    Strang 2016, Journal of Clinical Child & Adolescent Psychology · Consensus statement

  • Guidelines for the evaluation and treatment of perimenopausal depression: summary and recommendations

    Experts agree perimenopause raises depression risk. Standard treatments come first; estrogen may help some women, especially those with hot flushes.

    Maki 2018, Menopause · Guideline

  • Menopause hormone therapy and risk of mild cognitive impairment or dementia: a systematic review and meta-analysis

    Hormone therapy at menopause did not raise or lower the risk of dementia.

    Melville 2025, The Lancet Healthy Longevity · Meta-analysis

  • Methylphenidate and Atomoxetine in Pregnancy and Possible Adverse Fetal Outcomes

    Pooling ten studies, methylphenidate and atomoxetine in pregnancy were not linked to more birth defects or miscarriages.

    di Giacomo 2024, JAMA Network Open · Meta-analysis

  • Prevalence of attention‐deficit hyperactivity disorder in adult prisoners: An updated meta‐analysis

    About 1 in 12 adults in prison has ADHD, and the rate is similar in women and men.

    Fazel 2024, Criminal Behaviour and Mental Health · Meta-analysis

  • Investigating the Presence of Autistic Traits and Prevalence of Autism Spectrum Disorder Symptoms in Anorexia Nervosa: A Systematic Review and Meta‐Analysis

    People with anorexia had more autistic traits, and 29% scored above an autism cut-off.

    Inal‐Kaleli 2024, International Journal of Eating Disorders · Meta-analysis

  • Burden of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) in Indian Children: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

    Across Indian studies, 63.2 per 1000 children had ADHD on average, but estimates varied widely.

    Chauhan 2022, Indian Journal of Pediatrics · Meta-analysis

  • Autism Spectrum Disorder and Gender Dysphoria/Incongruence. A systematic Literature Review and Meta-Analysis

    Across studies, 11% of people with gender dysphoria had an autism diagnosis.

    Kallitsounaki 2022, Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders · Meta-analysis

  • Prevalence of Victimisation in Autistic Individuals: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

    Across studies, 44% of autistic people had been victimised and 40% had experienced sexual victimisation.

    Trundle 2022, Trauma, Violence, & Abuse · Meta-analysis

  • Prevalence and Risk Factors Associated With Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder Among US Black Individuals

    This review found ADHD was common among Black Americans, suggesting it may be missed rather than truly rarer.

    Cénat 2021, JAMA Psychiatry · Meta-analysis

  • The prevalence of adult attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder: A global systematic review and meta-analysis

    Worldwide, about 2.58% of adults have ADHD that started in childhood, and 6.76% have ADHD symptoms.

    Song 2021, Journal of Global Health · Meta-analysis

  • Child maltreatment and depression: A meta-analysis of studies using the Childhood Trauma Questionnaire

    Across nearly 69,000 people, emotional neglect and emotional abuse in childhood were among the experiences most strongly linked to later depression.

    Humphreys 2020, Child Abuse & Neglect · Meta-analysis
