Draft. Not yet reviewed by a clinician. Not medical advice.

In short

  • The evidence base on women with ADHD is held back less by a lack of studies than by how they are measured: retrospective cycle screens, borrowed masking scales, clinic samples, and results not split by sex.

  • Ten gaps stand out. The most consequential is that no ADHD × PMDD study has used prospectively confirmed diagnosis.

  • High-IQ and late-diagnosed women are largely missing from the data, and lived-experience input is rare outside autism research.

Evidence grade: Preliminary (P). This is a gaps register, so most of its content is about what is missing. Claims about measurement are graded individually and range from Established (E) to Preliminary (P). Design recommendations without a citation are labelled "Practical tip".

If you are thinking about suicide or are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a local crisis line now.

How to read this guide

Grades: [E] established, [S] supported, [P] preliminary, [U] popular but unsupported. Practical tip marks design advice that is reasonable but not itself evidence-based. The gap list draws on the research-gaps table in this project's publication plan, which in turn draws on the library's thread notes.

1. The 10 most important gaps

1. No prospectively confirmed PMDD in ADHD samples. In an online survey, provisional PMDD (PSST screen) was found in 31.4% of women with self-reported clinical ADHD vs 9.8% without ADHD Broughton 2025 [P]. In 2,000 employed Japanese women, ADHD traits were associated with PMDD (adjusted OR 6.49) on a retrospective scale Tsuji 2026 [P]. Provisional diagnosis inflates prevalence: 7.7% vs 3.2% with confirmed diagnosis in a meta-analysis Reilly 2024 [S]. Closing study: two or more cycles of DRSP with C-PASS scoring in clinically confirmed ADHD vs control women.

2. No RCT of cycle-adjusted stimulant dosing. The only evidence is a case series of 9 women de Jong 2023 [P]. Closing study: crossover RCT of a luteal-phase dose increase vs a placebo increase.

3. Perimenopause and HRT. One population cohort used self-reported ADHD; severe perimenopausal symptoms were more common with ADHD (54.2% vs 30.1%) Jakobsdóttir Smári 2025 [P]. A systematic review of ADHD and sex hormones found 11 small, heterogeneous studies and called for work on menopause Osianlis 2025 [P]. Closing study: cohort with clinically confirmed ADHD; HRT pilot.

4. No validated ADHD masking measure. See section 3.

5. "RSD" has never been validated. Rejection sensitivity is an established construct outside ADHD Downey 1996 [S]. In ADHD, the data are small and conflicting: no elevation in undergraduate men Canu 2007 [P], but higher rejection sensitivity in 10-19-year-olds with ADHD symptoms Bondü 2014 [P]. No study is in adult women. Closing study: validate rejection-sensitivity and emotion-regulation measures in women with ADHD across cycle phases.

6. Twice-exceptional adult women. Research on gifted students with disabilities suggests coexisting disability is real, but little is known about effective interventions Foley Nicpon 2010 [P]. The high-IQ adult ADHD studies are mixed-sex Antshel 2010 [S]. Closing study: sex-stratified analysis of high-IQ adult ADHD cohorts.

7. ADHD vs BPD misdiagnosis in women is unquantified. The overlap is well reviewed Asherson 2014 [S]; Ditrich 2021 [S]. No study gives a rate. Closing study: record-linkage of diagnostic trajectories.

8. Interoception in ADHD. Two small adult heartbeat studies disagree: a deficit in 14 adults with ADHD Kutscheidt 2019 [P] and none in 24 Wiersema 2018 [P]. A systematic review found 3 of 5 case-control studies showed reduced interoception, with small samples Bruton 2025 [P]. None analysed women separately. Closing study: a three-dimension design (accuracy, sensibility, awareness; Garfinkel 2015 [S]) in women with ADHD, controlling for cycle phase.

9. "ADHD burnout" has no construct. Adults with ADHD are more fatigued than controls (62% met fatigue caseness) Rogers 2016 [P], and one study of 171 employees links ADHD to job burnout via executive function Turjeman-Levi 2024 [P]. Burnout exhaustion correlates r = .80 with depressive symptoms Bianchi 2021 [S]. Closing study: measurement development with discriminant validity against depression.

10. Adult iron status in women with ADHD. The ferritin meta-analyses are in children (SMD -0.40) Wang 2017 [S]. The one adult study is RLS-focused, finding ferritin below 50 ng/mL in 35.5% Lopez 2019 [S]. Heavy menstrual bleeding is a major, under-recognised cause of iron deficiency Munro 2023 [S], but its link to ADHD symptoms is unstudied. Closing study: ferritin, bleeding and ADHD symptom cohort; iron RCT in women with low ferritin.

Further gaps (not ranked): no sex-stratified circadian data in adult ADHD; no meta-analysis of autism prevalence in adults with ADHD (the child estimate is 21% Hollingdale 2019 [E]); no definition of an "AuDHD" profile in women; adult endometriosis-ADHD evidence rests on one register study Gao 2020 [S].

2. Measurement: prospective DRSP/C-PASS, report sex, sample beyond clinics

Use prospective cycle ratings. The C-PASS scores DSM-5 PMDD from two or more months of daily DRSP ratings, with an estimated 98% correct classification against expert diagnosis; retrospective reports of premenstrual worsening poorly predicted prospective diagnosis Eisenlohr-Moul 2017 [S]. International consensus also calls for prospective quantification O'Brien 2011 [E]. Separate PMDD from premenstrual exacerbation (PME) of another disorder: around 60% of women with mood disorders report PME Kuehner 2021 [S].

Practical tip. Record hormonal contraception, stimulant timing and cycle day for every assessment, even in studies not about the cycle. Otherwise cycle phase may add unmeasured variance.

Report and test sex. A review of 73 studies concluded that many apparent gender differences in adult ADHD may be methodological artefacts or social influences Williamson 2015 [S]. In large studies, sex did not moderate ADHD's association with anxiety, depression or bipolar disorder Hartman 2023 [E]. Where sex does matter, it can change conclusions: some autoimmune associations appear in females only Hegvik 2018 [S], and medication was associated with fewer transport accidents in men but not significantly in women Chang 2014 [E].

Practical tip. Pre-register sex-stratified analyses. Report effect sizes by sex even when the interaction is non-significant, so later meta-analyses can pool them.

Sample beyond clinics. Referral is sex-biased: in 19,804 Swedish twins, hyperactivity and conduct problems predicted clinical diagnosis more strongly in girls Mowlem 2018 [S]. Ascertainment changes estimates: the ADHD-eating disorder odds ratio was 5.89 with clinical interview vs 2.23 with self-report Nazar 2016 [E], and provisional PMDD rates depended on recruitment Groenman 2026 [P]. Most adults with ADHD in a US national survey were untreated for it Kessler 2006 [E], so clinic samples miss many.

3. Masking measures: don't borrow the CAT-Q uncritically

The CAT-Q was built to measure camouflaging of autistic traits (details in this resource's questionnaire list). When it was applied to adults with ADHD, they scored above comparison adults but below autistic adults, and autistic traits, not ADHD traits, predicted camouflaging regardless of diagnosis van der Putten 2024 [P]. That suggests the instrument may be tracking autistic traits within ADHD samples. In autistic adults with social anxiety, CAT-Q scores did not predict outcomes beyond social responsiveness and social anxiety, suggesting overlapping constructs Roisenberg 2026 [P]. A systematic review called the camouflaging research base limited in participant characterisation and representativeness Cook 2021 [S].

ADHD-relevant compensation looks different. Adults with ADHD described organisational, motoric, attentional, social and pharmacological strategies before diagnosis Canela 2017 [P]. A transdiagnostic definition treats compensation as a mismatch between improved behaviour and persisting cognitive or neural differences Livingston 2017 [S], which implies measuring both levels, not self-report alone.

Practical tip. If you must use the CAT-Q in ADHD samples, measure and adjust for autistic traits, and report it as camouflaging of social traits, not "ADHD masking".

4. Include high-IQ and late-diagnosed women

Standard tests miss this group. Higher-IQ adults with ADHD differed from controls only on CPT commission errors, while standard-IQ adults were impaired on many tests Milioni 2016 [P]. Only 24% of correlations between EF tests and EF ratings were significant Toplak 2012 [E]. Compensatory strategies may raise the risk of a missed diagnosis at high IQ Rommelse 2016 [S].

Late diagnosis needs careful handling. In a cohort assessed eight times from age 10 to 25, about 95% of initial late-onset screen positives were excluded on closer assessment Sibley 2018 [S]. In autism, women had longer delays before referral and were more often misdiagnosed at first evaluation Gesi 2021 [P].

Practical tip. Don't exclude participants on an IQ ceiling. Include rating-based and informant EF measures alongside tests. Record age at diagnosis and prior diagnoses. Use full developmental histories to separate late recognition from late onset.

5. Lived-experience co-design

In our library, participatory methods appear in autism research but not in ADHD research. Autistic burnout was first defined through community-based participatory research Raymaker 2020 [P] and a Delphi panel of autistic adults Higgins 2021 [P]. We found no comparable ADHD studies in women, and no study testing whether co-design changes research quality.

Practical tip. Involve women with ADHD in choosing outcomes (for example, cycle-related function, daily-living load, fatigue), wording items and setting burden limits. Long daily-diary protocols may be hard to sustain with ADHD; pilot them for completion.

What to do in practice

  1. Use prospective DRSP with C-PASS for any premenstrual outcome. (cited above)
  2. Record contraception, cycle day and stimulant timing in every study. (Practical tip)
  3. Pre-register sex-stratified analyses and report effects by sex. (Practical tip)
  4. Recruit beyond clinics, and report recruitment route. (Practical tip)
  5. Don't treat the CAT-Q as an ADHD masking measure; adjust for autistic traits. (Practical tip)
  6. Drop IQ ceilings; add rating-based EF. (Practical tip)
  7. Build a lived-experience panel in from design onward. (Practical tip)

Talk to a clinician about…

  • Diagnostic confirmation. Partner with clinicians so ADHD (and PMDD) status is clinically confirmed, not self-reported.
  • Suicide-risk protocols. Women with PMDD had higher odds of suicide attempt (OR 6.97) and ideation (OR 3.95) Prasad 2021 [S], and clinically diagnosed premenstrual disorders were associated with higher suicide mortality (HR 1.92) Opatowski 2024 [S]. ADHD is also associated with suicidal behaviour Septier 2019 [E]. Daily-diary studies in this group need a clinician-approved risk protocol.
  • Medication changes during any trial involving cycle-timed dosing.

What we don't know yet

This guide is itself a list of unknowns. In addition: - We found no validated ADHD-specific camouflaging measure in the library. - We found no study testing lived-experience co-design in ADHD. - The library has no women-only adult ADHD cohort with cycle, iron, sleep and immune data together.

Popular claims checked

  • adhd-3x-more-likely-pmdd: "Women with ADHD are 3x more likely to have PMDD." Verdict: partly true (evidence grade: preliminary).
  • premenstrual-worsening-means-pmdd: "If your ADHD, anxiety or depression gets worse before your period, that's PMDD." Verdict: overstated (established).
  • women-mask-their-adhd: "Women with ADHD 'mask' their symptoms, and that's why they're diagnosed so late." Verdict: partly true (preliminary).
  • rsd-is-a-core-adhd-symptom: "RSD is a core part of ADHD." Verdict: unsupported (popular but unsupported).
  • adhd-means-poor-interoception: "People with ADHD have poor interoception." Verdict: unsupported (preliminary).
  • adhd-burnout-is-a-recognised-condition: "ADHD burnout is a recognised condition." Verdict: unsupported (popular but unsupported).
  • audhd-is-its-own-condition: "AuDHD is its own distinct condition." Verdict: unsupported (popular but unsupported).
  • adhd-in-women-is-a-different-disorder: "ADHD in women is a completely different disorder." Verdict: overstated (supported).
